About MedQA-Hindi

Project Overview

MedQA-Hindi is a model comparison and fine-tuning study for medical Q&A. It benchmarks a base Qwen model against QLoRA and DPO variants, tracking quality, alignment, and language control across Hindi and English.

Technology Stack

  • Base Model: Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct
  • Fine-tuning: QLoRA (4-bit NF4 + LoRA adapters)
  • Alignment: DPO (Direct Preference Optimization)
  • Translation: IndicTrans2 (English→Hindi)
  • Backend: FastAPI + Uvicorn
  • Frontend: Vanilla JS + Chart.js

Datasets

  • MedQuAD: 47K English medical QA pairs
  • MedMCQA-Indic: Hindi medical MCQs
  • HiMed: Hindi medical conversations
